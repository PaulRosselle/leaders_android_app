Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Leaders application handles information and files when you use the application.

1. Overview

Leaders is an offline Android application. The application is designed to operate locally on your device and does not require an account, registration, or an internet connection.

Leaders does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with the developer or with third parties.

2. Information We Collect

Leaders does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer or to third parties.

The application does not require you to provide your name, email address, phone number, location, account credentials, or any other personal information.

Leaders does not use advertising SDKs, analytics services, tracking technologies, or third-party services to collect information about your use of the application.

3. Files and Storage Access

Leaders may allow you to import or export puzzles and replay files in JSON format. Access to files is initiated explicitly by you using the standard Android file selection interface.

When you choose a file to import, Leaders accesses only the file that you explicitly select.

When you choose a folder for export, Leaders creates the exported file in the location that you explicitly select.

Leaders does not automatically scan your device, access your files without your action, or request broad access to your device storage.

4. How Files Are Used

Files selected by you are processed locally on your Android device for the purpose of importing or exporting puzzles or replays.

The contents of these files are not uploaded to a server and are not transmitted to the developer or to any third party by Leaders.

5. Data Sharing

Leaders does not share personal information or user files with third parties.

Because Leaders operates offline and does not transmit user data to external services, there is no third-party data sharing performed by the application.

6. Data Security

Leaders does not maintain a remote database containing user information. Data and files handled by the application remain on your device unless you choose to copy, export, or otherwise share them yourself using Android or another application.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Leaders does not retain personal information on external servers.

Files exported by the application to a location selected by the user remain in that location until the user deletes or moves them.

8. Children's Privacy

Leaders does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. The application does not require users to create accounts or provide personal information.

9. Internet Access

Leaders is designed to function offline and does not require an internet connection to use its core features.

The application does not send user data to remote servers.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the application or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
